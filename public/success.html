CRITICAL EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & HOUSEHOLD REPORT

Status: Offline Emergency Reference Guide

1. Action Checklists by Scenario

⚡ Power Blackout

  • Power Protection: Unplug sensitive electronics to prevent power surge damage when grid restores.
  • Food Preservation: Keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed. Unopened fridge keeps food cold for 4 hours; full freezer holds temperature for 48 hours.
  • Lighting & Safety: Use LED lanterns or flashlights. Avoid candles due to fire risk.

☣️ CBRN / Toxic Risks

  • Shelter-in-Place: Seal doors, windows, and air vents using duct tape and plastic sheeting.
  • Air Management: Turn off all HVAC systems, fans, and external air intakes immediately.

🔥 Disasters & Evacuation

  • Go-Bag Readiness: Ensure 72-hour emergency kits and essential identification documents are packed.
  • Evacuation Routes: Verify primary and secondary evacuation paths out of your immediate area.
