CRITICAL EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & HOUSEHOLD REPORT

Status: Offline Emergency Reference Guide

1. Action Checklists by Scenario

⚡ Power Blackout

  • Power Protection: Unplug sensitive electronics to prevent power surge damage when grid restores.
  • Food Preservation: Keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed. Unopened fridge keeps food cold for 4 hours; full freezer holds temperature for 48 hours.
  • Lighting & Safety: Use LED flashlights or headlamps. Avoid open candles to eliminate fire hazard.
  • Comms & Power: Switch phone to Maximum Power Saving mode. Use power banks only for critical updates.

☣️ CBRN Threat (Chemical, Biological, Radiological, Nuclear)

  • Immediate Shelter: Go indoors immediately. Close and lock all windows, exterior doors, and fireplace dampers.
  • Air Isolation: Turn off all HVAC systems, fans, and clothes dryers. Seal room gaps with wet towels or duct tape.
  • Positioning: For chemical threats, move to higher floors. For radiological threats, move to the center basement or interior room.
  • Decontamination: If exposed, remove outer clothing, seal in plastic bags, and wash skin thoroughly with soap and warm water.

🔥 Disaster Evacuation

  • Grab-and-Go: Collect 72-hour Bug-Out Bag, critical prescription medication, emergency cash, and identity documents.
  • Utility Shutdown: Shut off main gas valve, electricity breaker, and main water intake line before exiting.
  • Personal Protection: Wear sturdy shoes, long trousers, and protective long-sleeve clothing.
  • Route & Comms: Follow designated evacuation routes. Leave a written note inside indicating your departure time and destination.

⚔️ Conflict & Extreme Crisis

  • Hardened Shelter: Seek immediate shelter in an interior room without windows, below ground level if possible.
  • Light & Noise Blackout: Cover all windows with heavy blankets to eliminate light leaks at night. Keep sound to a minimum.
  • Essential Proximity: Keep clean drinking water, first aid supplies, and emergency radio within immediate physical reach.
  • Information Monitoring: Maintain battery/crank radio tuned to official emergency frequencies at scheduled intervals.

2. Immediate First Aid Procedures

  • Severe Bleeding: Apply firm, direct continuous pressure to the wound using sterile gauze or clean cloth. Elevate if possible. Do not remove saturated pads; add more on top.
  • Thermal/Chemical Burns: Cool burn immediately under cold running water for at least 10–20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments. Do not pop blisters. Cover loosely with sterile dressing.
  • Airway Obstruction (Choking): Perform 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades using the heel of your hand, followed by 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) until clear.
  • Shock Management: Lay victim flat on back, elevate feet 30 cm (12 inches) if no spinal injury is suspected, cover with emergency foil blanket to maintain body warmth, and reassure constantly.
